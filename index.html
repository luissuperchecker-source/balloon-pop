🎈 Balloon Pop!

Comparatives & superlatives – balloons float up into the sky. Shout the forms first and pop them for your team!

How to play: Balloons with adjectives float up from the ground. A student puts up their hand and says all three forms (e.g. big – bigger – biggest). Click the balloon – it pops and the game freezes. Choose the team, then mark the answer Right or Wrong. If it's wrong, the other team can steal the points. Balloons that fly away at the top are lost and appear on the practice list at the end.
Points: regular words (-er/-est, more/most) = 1 · spelling changes & irregular (bigger, happier, better…) = 2 · golden balloon = double.
Keys: 1/2 team · R right · W wrong · N nobody · S show answer · Esc undo pop · Space pause
